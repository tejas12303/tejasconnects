## Neon Snake

A small, dependency-free arcade game that runs in your browser. Open `index.html`
directly to play.

- Move with the arrow keys or **WASD**. On touch devices, use the direction pad.
- Press **Space** to pause or resume, and **Enter** to start or play again.
- Collect the glowing orbs to score points; the snake speeds up as your score grows.
- Your personal best is saved in your browser.
